Admission

Applications for the 2026–27 session are open. It takes about five minutes.

  1. Choose the programme you are applying for.
  2. Enter your personal, guardian and academic details.
  3. Save the application. You get an application number such as LAC-2026-0001.
  4. The admissions office reviews it and updates the status under Student records.
